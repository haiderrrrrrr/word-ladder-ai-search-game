Game Rules

Transform the start word into the target word by changing one letter at a time. Every intermediate word must be valid for the selected difficulty level.

Difficulty Levels

Beginner Mode: Uses 3-letter words and simpler transformations.

Advanced Mode: Uses 5-letter words and requires longer transformations.

Challenge Mode: Uses longer words, limited moves, and stricter scoring.

How To Play

1. Choose a difficulty level.

2. Change exactly one letter from the current word.

3. Submit only valid words from the dictionary.

4. Reach the target word before your score or moves run out.
